Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Purpose

This policy explains how Follent.net handles personal information in connection with its website services, and how you can ask questions or raise a concern.

Approach

You can browse our website without creating an account. Please share only the information needed to discuss your project. The website enquiry form is currently disabled, and Google Analytics is not enabled. You can contact us about privacy at privacy@follent.net.

Context & Authority

Follent.net is operated by James Follent. In this Privacy Policy, ‘we’, ‘us’ and ‘our’ refer to James Follent.

We use the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) as a best-practice guide. This statement does not claim that we have formally opted in to coverage under that Act.

Policy Statement #1 — Protecting your privacy and scope

1.1 This policy applies only to Follent.net and the handling of personal information in connection with this website and enquiries about its services.

1.2 You can read our pages without giving us your name. You can also use a different name for a general question. To reply, we need a working contact address. Agreeing to a project or accessing existing records can require your identity.

1.3 This website does not offer visitor accounts, public comments, image uploads or online checkout.

Policy Statement #2 — Personal information and collection

2.1 When you email us about privacy, you provide your email address, the contents of your message and any details or attachments you choose to include. Please avoid sending identity documents or sensitive information unless we have discussed a need for them.

2.2 The website enquiry form is not currently accepting submissions. Its required fields are your name, email address, project message and agreement to use those details to respond. Optional fields ask for your business name, phone number, existing website, type of help, package of interest and approximate timeline.

2.3 Please do not include passwords, payment card details, health information or other sensitive personal information in a project enquiry. Do not send another person’s details unless you have their permission or another appropriate basis to do so.

Policy Statement #3 — Storage, security and retention

3.1 The website enquiry form does not currently send or store submissions. Emailing us is separate from using that form: your email and our replies create correspondence records.

3.2 The website restricts which scripts and services its pages can load. The form is disabled until its privacy setup is complete. These measures reduce risks; they do not guarantee that information can never be lost or accessed without permission.

3.3 You can ask us about records we hold and request deletion using the contact in clause 8.1. Deletion is not an unrestricted right: a legal requirement to retain a record, or another applicable limitation, can affect what can be removed. We will explain the outcome of your request.

Policy Statement #4 — Use and disclosure

4.1 Information you send about privacy is used to understand your request, find relevant records, respond and keep a record of how the matter was handled.

4.2 The purpose of the project enquiry fields is to understand your needs, reply to you and discuss the scope of website services. The form’s agreement checkbox concerns that response; it is not a marketing subscription.

4.3 Formspark is the selected service for processing, storing and sending notifications of website enquiries. It is not receiving enquiries from the disabled form. It is a separate provider from the email service used when you email us directly.

Policy Statement #5 — Online activity and third-party services

5.1 Google Analytics, advertising tags, embedded social feeds and newsletter sign-up tools are not enabled on this website. The website’s own active scripts do not set analytics cookies or save an analytics preference on your device. This does not describe cookies or logs managed separately by a hosting or email provider.

5.2 Campaign tracking on the enquiry form is currently inactive. Its configured tool, Formtrack, attaches campaign labels from the Contact page’s web address to an enquiry. These are ref, referrer, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term and utm_content. The label “referrer” here means a value in that address, not a browsing history.

5.3 That configuration does not carry campaign labels between pages or save them in browser storage. Formtrack is supplied by the unpkg script delivery service and is not loaded while the form is disabled. Please keep personal information out of campaign links.

5.4 Following a link to an external website takes you to a service with its own information handling practices. Read that service’s privacy notice before giving it personal information.

Policy Statement #6 — Overseas handling

6.1 The disabled website form does not send enquiries overseas. The location of a provider and the location of stored information are different matters.

6.2 Formspark is operated from Belgium. Its published policy identifies Ireland and Germany as storage locations. Its provider list also identifies OOPSpam in the United States for spam checks, a feature that can be switched off. These are provider disclosures, not a statement that the disabled form is sending your information to those countries.

6.3 You can read the Formspark privacy policy and its service provider list for details. Overseas storage or processing does not, by itself, establish whether a particular arrangement is a disclosure under Australian privacy law.

Policy Statement #7 — Access, correction and preferences

7.1 Email privacy@follent.net to request access to personal information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct it. Describing the relevant interaction helps us find the right records. You do not need to use a special form.

7.2 Before releasing or changing records, we will check that the request is from you or someone authorised to act for you. We will ask only for details reasonably needed for that check. If access or correction is limited, including to protect another person’s privacy or meet a legal requirement, we will explain why and how you can raise a concern.

7.3 You can tell us how you prefer to be contacted or ask us to stop promotional messages at the same email address. A privacy request or project enquiry does not sign you up to a mailing list. We can still respond to your request and send communications needed for an agreed service.

Policy Statement #8 — Privacy enquiries and complaints

8.1 Contact James Follent at privacy@follent.net with a privacy question or complaint, including a concern about a service provider’s handling of your information. Please describe what happened and the outcome you are seeking.

8.2 We will acknowledge your complaint, review the relevant information, ask for clarification where needed and explain our findings and any proposed steps to resolve it. If more time is needed, we will keep you informed. You can reply if you disagree or have further information.

8.3 If the matter remains unresolved, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner’s complaint guidance explains its process and eligibility rules. The OAIC can consider complaints within its jurisdiction; it does not investigate every business or privacy concern.

8.4 This policy is available to read free of charge. Email us if you need it in another format, such as plain text or large print. We will discuss a suitable format and take reasonable steps to provide it.

Policy Statement #9 — Changes to this policy

9.1 We will review this policy when our information handling changes, including before enabling enquiry collection or optional tracking. The revision date at the top identifies the version you are reading.

9.2 The current policy will be available at this address. Information given alongside a particular collection explains that interaction; this policy does not replace a collection notice where one is required.
